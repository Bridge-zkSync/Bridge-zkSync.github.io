How the estimate works

The fee is 0.6% of the amount plus 0.50 units, rounded to two decimals. The received amount is the input minus this fee, with a 1:1 unit conversion. Timing is 2–6 minutes plus one minute for each 1,000 units, rounded up and capped at 10 extra minutes.

Bringing assets in with the zkSync Bridge

The zkSync Bridge deposits your asset into an Ethereum contract and mints it on zkSync Era, where it lives inside a smart account rather than a plain address. That account abstraction is native, so from the first transaction you can batch actions, set spending rules and use features ordinary EOAs cannot.

Paymasters: paying zkSync Bridge fees without ETH

Because accounts are programmable, a paymaster can sponsor your gas or let you pay fees in an ERC-20 instead of ETH. After you bridge in, some apps cover the first transactions or accept USDC for gas, which lowers the barrier compared with a normal rollup where only ETH works.

zkSync Bridge proofs and withdrawal timing

zkSync Era proves its batches to Ethereum with validity proofs, so state is verified rather than trusted. Deposits credit within minutes; a standard withdrawal waits for the proof to finalize on Ethereum, which is why exiting takes longer than entering. The estimate is shown before you confirm.

What a zkSync Bridge transfer costs

You pay Ethereum gas to deposit; on zkSync Era execution is cheap, and a paymaster can shift who pays. Judge a transfer by the net amount received after the Ethereum gas and any route fee, and avoid bridging tiny amounts where gas dominates.

Using the zkSync Bridge on zkSync Era

With a smart account you get session keys, batched swaps and gas paid in tokens across zkSync DeFi. Keep a little ETH unless an app sponsors gas, and withdraw through the canonical route when you want native ETH back on Ethereum.
What is account abstraction on zkSync?
On zkSync Era every account is a smart contract, so wallets can batch actions, use paymasters and set custom rules natively.
Can I pay zkSync fees without ETH?
Often yes — a paymaster can sponsor gas or let you pay in an ERC-20 such as USDC, depending on the app.
Why does a zkSync withdrawal take longer than a deposit?
Deposits credit after Ethereum confirmations; withdrawals wait for a validity proof to finalize on Ethereum first.
Do I need an account to use the zkSync Bridge?
No sign-up — you connect a self-custody wallet and bridge on-chain, keeping custody of your funds.
What if my deposit fails?
It reverts and you keep your assets; only the Ethereum gas for the attempt is spent.
